Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How fast does the JanSeva team respond to emergency requests?

Emergency requests are reviewed within 1 to 3 hours. Field team verification takes place within 24 hours.

What is Childline 1098?

Childline 1098 is India's 24x7 toll-free emergency phone service for children in need of care and protection.

Are services on JanSeva completely free?

Yes! All social help, legal counseling, child protection, and ration drives provided on this platform are 100% free.
